Privacy Policy

Quest Tracker for Raiders · Last updated: September 21, 2026
Quest Tracker for Raiders respects your privacy. The app does not require a user account. Some information may be processed by third-party services used for advertising and event data, as described below.

Information Stored on Your Device

Quest progress, tracking preferences, workshop-related state, language settings, consent choices, and other app preferences may be stored locally on your device. This information is used to provide app functionality.

External Data Services

The app connects to MetaForge to retrieve game event schedule data. Requests to MetaForge may be subject to MetaForge's own server logging and privacy practices. Quest Tracker for Raiders does not use this connection to create user profiles.

MetaForge: metaforge.app

Advertising

Quest Tracker for Raiders uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information in order to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

Depending on device settings, consent choices, applicable law, and the type of ad served, information processed by Google Mobile Ads may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Device identifiers, including advertising or app-bounded identifiers where available.
  • Advertising data, such as ads shown to the user.
  • App and ad interaction data, such as launches, taps, and video views.
  • Crash, diagnostic, and performance information.

The exact data processed may vary depending on the user's privacy choices, device configuration, region, and Google's current advertising systems.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Consent and Privacy Choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising privacy choices where required by applicable privacy regulations.

Depending on the user's region, available options may include consenting to advertising data processing, declining consent, managing individual privacy choices, or opting out of the sale or sharing of personal data.

Users can revisit applicable advertising privacy choices through the Advertising Privacy Settings option in the app's Settings screen when that option is required.

Personalized and Non-Personalized Advertising

Advertising may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or otherwise restricted depending on the user's consent choices, device settings, geographic region, and applicable legal requirements. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that no ads will be shown.

Device Advertising Identifier

On iOS, access to the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is controlled by Apple's privacy framework. Where IDFA access is available and permitted, it may be used by advertising services for purposes such as ad delivery, measurement, attribution, and fraud prevention. If access is not available or permission is not granted, advertising may still be delivered using other permitted signals.

Personal Information

The app does not require registration and does not ask users to provide names, email addresses, contacts, photos, health information, financial information, or precise location. Third-party advertising services may process technical and advertising-related information as described above.

Data Retention and Deletion

App data stored locally remains on the device until it is reset within the app or the app is deleted. Quest Tracker for Raiders does not maintain a developer-controlled user account or remote database containing local quest progress.

Data processed by Google Mobile Ads, UMP, or MetaForge may be retained according to those providers' own privacy policies and legal obligations.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — advertising delivery and related measurement.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — advertising consent and privacy choices.
  • MetaForge — game event schedule data.

Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children. Advertising and privacy choices are handled through the third-party systems described in this policy.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features, third-party services, or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent through the support contact listed on the app's App Store page.

Unofficial App Notice

Quest Tracker for Raiders is an independently developed, unofficial companion app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the developer or publisher of the related game.
